Dictate here

Just a dump of what is being said. No account, no Google Doc, no append.

Tap the microphone to start
Speak in full sentences and say punctuation out loud — “period,” “comma,” “new paragraph.”

Android: use Chrome, allow the mic, and tap the red button. iPhone / iPad: the in-page mic is not supported in Safari or Chrome on iOS, so tap the transcript box and use your keyboard’s mic button to dictate instead — Copy and Download still work.

How this works: it is just your browser’s built-in speech recognition. No sign-in, no minutes, no Google Doc, and nothing is sent to a server by this page except what Chrome’s own speech service needs to transcribe.
